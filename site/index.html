windsor

A Ford 302 V8, written in C++. It has no graphics and it moves nothing. It turns over, burns fuel that does not exist, and sounds like this.

idlecruisepulloverrun

Nothing in it is a sample. Every pressure wave in those nine seconds was computed, from a bore, a stroke, a camshaft and a crankshaft. Press play, then fit the other crank while it runs.

An engine, written down

The source is meant to be read the way a shop manual is. You give it what Ford's drawings give, in the units Ford used, and a bore is a different type from a stroke, so the compiler will not let you swap them.

Why a Mustang does not sound like a Ferrari

Both are V8s, and both fire evenly, once every 90° of rotation. The difference is one part. Look down the nose of the crankshaft and its four throws make either a cross or a line. This is the program's own drawing of the one fitted now:

An engine does not exhale through its flywheel. It exhales through two exhaust manifolds, and each one hears only its own four cylinders. This is when each bank's cylinders fire, over two cycles:

right bank
left bank

On the cross, a bank coughs twice in quick succession, waits three quarters of a turn, and coughs again. That limp is the burble. Now fit the flat crank.

On the flat crank each bank breathes evenly. The limp is gone, and the burble went with it. Same block, same cam, same nine seconds.

Nobody typed in the firing order

This is the whole crankshaft as the source describes it: a bank angle, four throws, and eight rods. It is what a foundry decides, and nothing more.

1-5-4-2-6-3-7-8 is cast into the intake manifold of every 302 Ford built in 1968. It is not an input here. The program is handed the forging and the order falls out, and the limp with it, and the sound with that.

And it makes the power Ford said it did

Why

To find out whether the burble falls out of the geometry or has to be put there by hand. It falls out. What that left behind is an engine that runs in a terminal and cannot move anything, and does so correctly. It is not useful. It is the most careful thing I have made.
