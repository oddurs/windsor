windsor

A Ford 302, modelled from first principles in C++23, for the sound.

Press play, then change the crankshaft while it runs.

A 90° V8 fires evenly on either crankshaft: every 90°, eight times a cycle. But an engine exhales through two manifolds, and each one hears only its own four cylinders. On a cross-plane crank a bank coughs twice, waits three quarters of a turn, and coughs again. On a flat one it breathes evenly. That is the whole difference between a Mustang and a Ferrari, and nobody typed it in. The firing order is not an input. It falls out of the forging.

make && ./windsor
